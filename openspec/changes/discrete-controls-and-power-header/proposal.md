## Why
Mode and fan controls need discrete sliders, and the power header clips on constrained screens.

## What Changes
- Use compact supported-value sliders that commit on release.
- Reflow the shared power header for narrow and enlarged layouts.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `discrete-control-presentation`: slider and power-header presentation.

## Impact
Shared Compose controls, Home and Device presentation, and Android tests.
