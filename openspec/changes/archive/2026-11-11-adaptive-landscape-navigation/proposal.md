## Why

Bottom navigation and narrow columns leave little usable height in a wide landscape window.

## What Changes

- Move navigation to a compact side rail in sufficiently wide landscape windows.
- Use available width for side-by-side instrument controls and the energy chart when text scale and space permit.
- Keep narrow windows and enlarged text readable with a stacked layout.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities
- `mobile-experience`: adaptive navigation and instrument layout.

## Impact

The production Compose shell, Home instrument, and layout tests.
